Our story

Rooted in the neighborhood, focused on your yard.

Luke & Will’s Yardwork began as two friends helping out on weekends—mowing lawns, trimming edges, and cleaning up leaves for neighbors who needed an extra hand. Word spread, and before long, those weekend favors grew into a trusted local service.

Today, we still carry that same neighbor‑first mindset. We show up when we say we will, we pay attention to the details, and we leave your yard looking better than we found it—every single time.

Whether you need a one‑time cleanup or ongoing care, our goal is simple: make your outdoor space something you’re proud of, without the stress of doing it all yourself.

Services

Detailed Offerings

Lawn Mowing

We provide professional mowing for both standard residential lawns and larger properties. We utilize a riding mower to efficiently handle pastures and expansive lawns, and a push mower for smaller yards and tight spaces. Our service includes precision trimming around edges and obstacles to give your property a clean, manicured look.

Raking

Don't let fallen debris suffocate your grass. We offer thorough raking and removal of leaves and pine needles, ensuring your lawn stays healthy and clear throughout the changing seasons.

Weeding

We tackle stubborn weeds in gardens, walkways, and driveways. We pay close attention to detail to ensure that even the smallest weeds are removed from the cracks and soil, keeping your outdoor spaces looking pristine.

Watering

Keeping your plants and lawn hydrated is essential for a vibrant property. We offer reliable watering services for your garden, lawn, and flower beds to ensure everything stays green and healthy.

Snow Plowing

When the winter weather hits, we're ready. We provide reliable snow removal for driveways and walkways, helping you keep your property accessible and safe for your family and vehicles.

Animal Watching

Need someone reliable to check on your pets or livestock? We offer animal watching services 7 days a week, providing feeding, watering, and general care so you can have peace of mind while you're away.

About Us

Our Story

Who We Are

In 2026, our parents told us that we needed to stay busy. To accomplish this goal and make money at the same time, we decided to start our own company helping neighbors out. We are two boys who love the outdoors and like to do a solid day's work. We believe that our character is formed through hard work, and we believe that responsibility is an important part of growing up.

We have decided to do lawn care and snow removal not just because we need money, but because we believe people should be able to have the chance to get their lawn cleaned and snow removed at a low price. We especially want to do this for people who are older or busy and do not have the chance or strength to clean their lawns.

What We Do

Detailed Services

Mowing & Yard Work

  • House Lawns: Full mowing, weed-eating, and blowing of walkways.
  • Pastures & Fields: Tractor mowing for large acreage and roadside frontage.
  • General Yard Care: Raking, rock removal, and debris hauling.
  • Garden Care: Weeding fence lines, gravel driveways, flower beds, and watering services.

Snow Removal

  • Driveway Plowing: Standard clearing and "Big Storm" management (2+ feet).
  • Hand Shoveling: Safe clearing of porches, walkways, and decks.

Animal Care & Farm Bundles

  • Small Animals: Indoor dogs, cats, and household pets.
  • Large Animals: Horses, cows, goats, and other livestock.
  • Farm Bundle: Comprehensive care for all animals on your property.

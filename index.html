Deep Dive: Phase 01

The Contact Firewall

Dealerships are built on "lead follow-up." Once they have your phone number, you are a data point in a CRM designed to call you three times a day until you buy or die. We stop this.

By acting as your professional representative, we vet every offer. If a dealer doesn't have the car or isn't willing to talk numbers over the phone, we discard them immediately so you never hear about it.

Deep Dive: Phase 02

The Financial Audit

A dealership makes its most profit in the "F&I" (Finance and Insurance) office. This is where hidden markups, warranties you don't need, and high-interest rate spreads are added.

We secure a written Buyer's Order before you leave your house. We audit every line item. If we see a $500 "Admin Fee" or a $2,000 "Appearance Package," we negotiate it off or we walk. We ensure your monthly payment is based on the actual math, not a dealer's "rounded up" estimate.

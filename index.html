Our Specialized Care

Detailed Offerings

Mowing Services

House Lawns: Our standard lawn service includes thorough weed-eating and blowing off surfaces for a clean finish.

Pastures & Fields: We handle larger areas and roadside frontage with our riding equipment.

General Yard Work

Cleanup: Comprehensive raking, rock removal, and debris clearing to keep your land pristine.

Weeding: Specialized attention to fence lines, gravel areas, and side yards.

Garden Care: Expert maintenance for flower beds and vegetable gardens.

Watering: Daily watering for gardens, pots, or individual trees.

Snow Plowing & Shoveling

We use shovels and plows (no blowers) to ensure a quiet and effective clearing.

Driveways: Available for standard snowfalls and "Big Storm" events (2+ feet).

Decks & Walks: Hand-shoveling for porches, walks, and decks to keep your access points safe.

Animal Care (7 Days/Week)

Small Animals: Daily care for cats, dogs, and other indoor pets.

Large Animals: Experienced handling of cows, horses, goats, and more.

Farm Bundle: A comprehensive flat-rate service covering all animals on your property.
